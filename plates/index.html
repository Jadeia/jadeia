On provenance

Nothing in this catalogue was made for it. Each plate is reproduced from the holding institution’s own open-access release — the Metropolitan Museum of Art under CC0, the University of Aberdeen for the bestiary folio — and each is credited in full above. Where a date is given as ca., the uncertainty belongs to the scholarship and not to me.

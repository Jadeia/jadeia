    What the Twelfth Century Believed

    A monk seven hundred years dead, entirely wrong about the anatomy and entirely right about the patience.

    Plate I — Aberdeen Bestiary, folio 65v
  2. Chronicle II

    Saint George, So Many Times

    Nuremberg cut the same scene until it stopped being a scene and became a habit.

    Plate II — Dürer, Saint George and the Dragon

    The Other Slayer

    Seven heads, borrowed crowns, and stakes that are at least honestly stated.

    Plate III — Dürer, Saint Michael Fighting the Dragon

    Before the Fight

    Everyone is brave once the beast is already dying. This plate is about the hour before that.

    Plate IV — Dürer, Saint George Standing

    On Keeping These at All

    The question I am asked rarely, and the answer I give instead of the true one.

    Plate V — Schongauer, Saint George Slaying the Dragon
